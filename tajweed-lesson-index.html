The Purpose of Tajweed

🔤 Imagine you have a twin brothers or friends? 🔤

Tom
VS
Tim

They sound alike, but they're DIFFERENT! Just like Arabic twin letters!


Taa (ت) and Taa (ط) sound similar, but swapping them changes the word completely! 🎯

📖 A Real Qur'anic Example! 📖

قُلْ هُوَ اللَّهُ أَحَدٌ

(From Surah Al-Ikhlas)

Qul (قُلْ)
Heavy ق Sound
Say! 📢
VS
Kul (كُلْ)
Light ك Sound
Eat! 🍕

Imagine saying 'EAT: He is Allah' instead of 'SAY: He is Allah'! 😱


This is why Tajweed is SO IMPORTANT! Every letter must be perfect! ✨👑

🔍 Deducing the Rule! 🔍

What is the MAIN PURPOSE of studying Tajweed?

🏠 Every Letter Has an Exit Point! 🏠

Makhraj (Exit point): The exact spot where the letter is born (throat, tongue, lips)! 🏠


Sifaat (Attributes): Is the letter heavy like ط or light like ت? 💪✨


Change the exit point = Change the word! عَلِيمٌ (All-Knowing) vs أَلِيمٌ (Painful)! 🎯

🔤 One Small Vowel Changes EVERYTHING! 🔤

ٱلَّذِينَ أَنْعَمْتَ عَلَيْهِمْ

(From Surah Al-Fatihah)

تَ (Fatha)
◌َ (Open)
YOU gave blessings! 👊
VS
تُ (Damma)
◌ُ (Round)
I gave blessings! 👉

One small vowel = Completely different meaning! That's why Tajweed matters! ✨👑
